The frame

The chassis

I own the chassis architecture: vehicle structure, ergonomics, and the driver interface and safety systems. I also coordinate packaging across teams so every subsystem fits.

Torsional stiffness model

A MATLAB model for specifying a torsional stiffness target, built alongside ANSYS models of how chassis compliance affects lateral load transfer. It let us refine the targets and take weight out of the frame.

Custom fixtures

I introduced fixture-based frame manufacturing to the team, designing and producing 100+ precision tube fixtures.

Frame metrics

  • Chassis subsystem design points up 27.7%.
  • Frame weight down 10%.
  • Welding time cut 50% at ±3 mm frame geometry accuracy.
