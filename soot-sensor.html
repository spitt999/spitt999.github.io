Low-cost soot concentration sensor

I engineered the full mechanical design: a sliding filter-tape mechanism for air sampling, with automated particle analysis.

  • A servo-actuated lifting system and custom gasket-sealed tubing, integrated with a vacuum pump and digital microscope.
  • 99% lower cost than commercial instruments, with comparable accuracy and precision.
